# MIKE searching

## Purpose

Run the complete FlavorWise partnership-development system from finding the right object to learning from the outcome:

`Research → Classify → Score → Bias Shield → Forecast → Decision → Outreach → Follow-up → Deal → Outcome → Learning`

Use it for Creator, KOL, Media, Grower, Commercial Grower, Distributor, Retailer, Hydro Store, Equipment Dealer, Consultant, and Strategic Partner work. Optimize first for attributable Revenue, Contribution Profit, Repeat Orders, Account Value, LTV, and channel value; treat followers, views, replies, meetings, and leads as intermediate signals.

This skill extracts a reusable method, not a customer database. It must never contain or retrieve real customer names, personal contact details, private messages, account histories, shipping addresses, confidential prices, or client-specific decisions. Use placeholders such as `[Company]`, `[Name]`, `[Country]`, and `[Content Anchor]` in reusable outputs.

## When to activate

Activate for requests equivalent to:

- find, screen, research, compare, or prioritize a creator, media outlet, grower, retailer, distributor, dealer, or other B2B account;
- write a first outreach email, WhatsApp message, LinkedIn message, or localized version;
- classify a reply, decide whether and when to follow up, or move an opportunity through a pipeline;
- evaluate a test unit, sample, sponsorship, paid placement, affiliate, reseller, distribution, pilot, or other partnership;
- review a deal, forecast probabilities, calculate expected value, record an outcome, or improve the system.

Do not use this skill to send a message, publish content, pay money, change a live CRM, promise commercial terms, or delete records unless the user separately authorizes that action.

## Operating rules

1. Separate verified fact, self-report, third-party information, inference, draft, unknown, and stale-risk information.
2. Prefer first-party sources and current evidence. Attach a source URL and publication/verification date to every material fact, Content Anchor, role, business claim, and commercial condition. Before external action, recheck the latest thread, website, role, contact route, product material, price, stock, freight, warranty, and timing.
3. Keep Forecast and Decision separate. A high forecast does not authorize an investment; a low forecast does not prove rejection when evidence is missing.
4. Do not turn willingness to collaborate, prestige, company size, follower count, traffic, or a positive reply into proof of commercial value.
5. If the evidence needed to make a safe decision is missing, say `WAIT FOR EVIDENCE` and name the exact variable to verify.
6. Standardize backend research, scoring, forecasts, records, and retrospectives. Personalize the external message and relationship handling.
7. Never generalize a rule from one customer, one campaign, or one failure without recording the evidence and testing the hypothesis.
8. Apply a legal/market gate before outreach or investment. If the market, recipient's business activity, or proposed use is not legally and operationally clear, say `WAIT FOR EVIDENCE`; never design a way around regulation.
9. Treat `APPROVE` as approval of the next internal step only. It is not approval to send, pay, ship a machine, grant a discount, sign, or promise terms unless separately authorized.

## Required output shape

For a named object, provide only the sections relevant to the request, in this order when a full analysis is requested:

1. Identity, archetype, geography, language, and current stage.
2. Evidence: verified facts, self-reported facts, third-party facts, inference, unknown, and stale-risk items.
3. Revenue/Authority or Revenue/Strategic scores with evidence and confidence; do not manufacture missing points.
4. Bias Shield: base rate, at least three verified disconfirming signals when available, missing variables, premortem, and opportunity cost. Never present an absence of evidence as a negative fact.
5. Forecast probabilities and separate Cash EV/Contribution Profit EV from Strategic or Authority upside; use ranges and confidence when evidence is thin.
6. Decision: `APPROVE`, `NEGOTIATE`, `WAIT`, or `REJECT`, with the reason and the next smallest useful action.
7. Outreach or follow-up draft in the recipient's working language plus a complete Chinese review translation when the draft is not Chinese.
8. CRM-ready update using generic fields only: stage, last interaction, next action, owner, due date, forecast change, decision, and failure reason if applicable.

Do not include private client information in reusable skill files, examples, templates, or generated documentation. Remove real identifiers before exporting this skill or sharing it in a repository.
